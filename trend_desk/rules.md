# Trend Desk: Meagan's standing rules

Every agent in Trend Desk (Judge, Router, outline writer, brief room) reads this file on every
run. Rules come from Meagan, by text to the Trend Desk bot or in a Claude session. Add new rules
at the bottom with the date; never delete one, mark it "(retired YYYY-MM-DD)" instead.

- 2026-10-25 · Volume is "as much as makes sense": a quality bar, not a quota.
- 2026-10-25 · Any of her sites can get a brief.
- 2026-10-25 · TermScope is never the final say on a brief's structure. It supplies research;
  the brief room's agents decide the structure together.
- 2026-10-25 · She approves finished work only. Nothing is sent, launched or published by Trend Desk.
